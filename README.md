## About me

I got into software by building tools for problems I ran into at work. Before development, I worked in sales and operations, including supervising field and sales teams, and that is still how I approach a project: understand the process first, then write the code.

Most of my work today is on the back end and data side, with **PostgreSQL**, **Supabase** and **Node.js**, and **React** on the front end. I also use **n8n** and **Playwright** a lot to automate workflows and connect systems that were never meant to talk to each other.

I'm studying **Information Systems at FACIMP Wyden** (expected 2028) and looking for internship or junior roles in development, automation or data.
